    Frequently asked questions

    What is Button Counter?

    Button Counter gives you a view of your financial future: how long your savings will last and what changes when you adjust something. You enter your age, your savings and what you want to spend each month. Button Counter then shows the chance that your money lasts until your end age, based on 4,000 simulations of good and bad market years.

    How much savings do I need to stop working?

    That depends on your monthly budget, your age and the pension you will receive. Button Counter calculates the amount you need for a 90% chance that your money is enough. As a rough rule of thumb, people often need 25 to 30 times the yearly spending that their pensions do not cover.

    How is the chance of success calculated?

    Button Counter runs 4,000 simulations in which returns go up and down each year. It does this for three portfolios (defensive, balanced and aggressive), after costs, tax and inflation, so every amount is in today’s money. The chance of success is the share of simulations in which your money lasts until your end age. Two crash scenarios, one like 2008 and one like 2022, are shown separately.

    Which countries does Button Counter support?

    Button Counter has its own rules for the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Ireland, the United Kingdom and the United States, plus a general setting for other countries. Each country has its own state pension, pension age and tax on investments. Button Counter also compares what your budget is worth in 31 European countries, based on Eurostat price levels.

    Is Button Counter free, and what happens to my data?

    At the moment Button Counter is free to use, has no advertising and needs no account. What you enter stays in your own browser on your own device and is not sent to Button Counter. Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. Button Counter is a general calculation tool that gives estimates based on simplified rules and assumptions. It does not give advice about your personal situation. Check official sources and consult an independent, licensed adviser before you make important financial decisions.

    Privacy

    Your data stays with you. What you enter (ages, amounts, choices) is stored only in your own browser on this device. It is not sent to Button Counter and nobody else can see it. If you clear your browser data, it is gone.

    No tracking. No advertising cookies are used and you are not followed. Visits are counted anonymously (only the number of visits, pages and country) with GoatCounter, without cookies and without personal data. The storage in your browser only makes the site work and remembers your input.

    What does run through others. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees technical data such as your IP address when the page loads. The form behind “Share a tip” is a Google Form: what you write there reaches the maker and possibly Google. Don’t enter anything there that you want to keep private. Links to sources lead to other websites with their own rules.

    Contact. Button Counter is a personal, non-commercial project. What you send through “Share a tip” is used only to improve the site and is not kept longer than needed. Want something deleted, or have a question about this text? Send a message through “Share a tip”.
